WikiChat — Search Wikipedia Naturally

Search Wikipedia naturally, discover related articles, and explore knowledge through a futuristic conversational interface.

What is WikiChat?

WikiChat is a free conversational Wikipedia search engine designed to make discovering encyclopedia information simple. Search for people, places, science, history, technology, culture, geography, and thousands of other topics.

Search Wikipedia Naturally

Instead of navigating complicated menus, type a question or topic into WikiChat. The application searches Wikipedia's public API and presents a relevant article with a summary, image, related articles, and a link to the original Wikipedia page.

WikiChat Features

  • Fast Wikipedia search
  • Natural-language searches
  • Related article discovery
  • Wikipedia article reader
  • Local search history
  • Saved articles and bookmarks
  • Multiple Wikipedia languages
  • Dark and light themes
  • Mobile-friendly interface
  • Shareable search links

Search Wikipedia in Multiple Languages

WikiChat supports searches through several Wikipedia language editions, including English, Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Arabic, Spanish, French, and German.

Free Wikipedia Search Tool

WikiChat uses Wikipedia's public API directly from your browser. There is no custom application backend required for searches, while your local history and saved articles remain stored in your browser.
